WHY THIS ISN'T JUST ABOUT ONE CAMERA

The decision is local. The reach isn't.

Every one of those questions has a local answer: your community, your department, your contract.

But a city council voting on cameras is a real, local decision, made by people you can call or vote against. Once those cameras join a searchable, shareable network, what was purchased can end up reaching other departments, other states, sometimes federal agencies, without anyone in that community ever voting on that broader reach specifically.

That gap, between the local decision and the network it joins, is the reason this site exists.
