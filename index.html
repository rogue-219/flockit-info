KNOW WHO'S WATCHING

What do they know about you?

Flock cameras don't simply take pictures of license plates. They are part of a searchable surveillance network capable of recording vehicle movements and helping authorities determine where a vehicle has been.

Your community may already be using them. We think you should know.

WHY IT MATTERS

This is bigger than a license plate camera.

Automated license plate readers can be useful law-enforcement tools. But a network of cameras that records vehicle movements, stores searchable information, and allows that information to be accessed across agencies raises much bigger questions.

What is being collected? How long is it kept? Who can search it? Who can it be shared with? What safeguards exist against misuse? And who decided those rules?

Why You Should Care

FOLLOW THE CONTRACT

What did your community agree to?

Flockit.info is building a public record of government agreements involving Flock surveillance technology.

We look for the contract, cost, funding source, approval process, policies, contract term, and other information that helps residents understand what their government purchased and how it can be used.

Contract

What was purchased, how much it costs, and who approved it.

Policies

Retention, access, sharing, hot-list use, auditing, and other safeguards we can document.

Renewal

When the agreement ends, whether it renews automatically, and when action may need to begin before renewal.

THE DATE THAT MATTERS

When does your community's contract renew?

A contract expiration date isn't very useful if the agreement requires months of advance notice to stop renewal.

That's why Flockit.info will highlight not only the renewal date, but also the window when residents and elected officials may need to act.
