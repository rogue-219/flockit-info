WHY IT MATTERS

A license plate reader can tell a much bigger story.

Flock cameras are often described as license plate readers. But the plate is only one piece of information a system can collect about a vehicle. When cameras are networked and their records are searchable, individual detections can become a history of where a vehicle has been seen.

It’s not just about the plate.

Modern vehicle-recognition systems can capture more than a license plate number. Depending on the technology and configuration, a detection may include details such as vehicle make, model, color, distinguishing features, location, and time. Those details can make a vehicle searchable even when the plate itself is unknown.
