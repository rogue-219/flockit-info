Michigan City

Additional records received

City of La Porte: Public records include an INDOT agreement and a permit packet containing a signed 2023 Flock order for 11 cameras. The order specifies a 60-month initial term and 24-month renewals. Current deployment, payment history, and applicable later amendments have not been independently established.

LaPorte County Sheriff's Office: Records include Policy 426, six distinct invoice numbers, an eight-entry installed-camera tracker dated September 23, 2026, and sharing, usage-category, and audit-event exports. The exports do not by themselves establish investigation outcomes or the full contractual terms. The agency has indicated its production is complete; the available documents still leave questions about retention settings, contract terms, and funding.

These are summaries of records reviewed, not findings that every contracted camera is currently operational. Supporting document downloads and dedicated profiles are being prepared for publication.
