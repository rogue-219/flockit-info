COMMUNITY RESEARCH GUIDE

Start researching your community.

Community submissions to FlockIt.info are currently unavailable. This page does not send a request to us or to a government agency. You can use the worksheet below to prepare your own public-records inquiry.

A worksheet you can keep and edit yourself.

Download the blank text file, edit it in your own text editor, and choose the records relevant to your question. There are no visitor-information fields or submission controls on this page.

Download the records-request worksheet

  1. Identify the city, county, or agency and the time period you want to research.
  2. Choose specific existing records, such as orders, amendments, policies, invoices, or records establishing installation and validation dates.
  3. Find the agency's published records instructions on its official website. Check the recipient and submission method before sending anything.
  4. Submit through that official channel if you choose, and keep your request, acknowledgment, and responses together. Downloading the worksheet sends no records request.

Public-records laws and disclosure exceptions vary. An agency may redact or withhold some records; a missing record or unanswered question alone does not establish wrongdoing. Do not include sensitive personal information that is unnecessary to your request.
