REQUEST A COMMUNITY

What should we investigate next?

Tell us which city, county, or government agency you want Flockit.info to look into. We'll work to identify and obtain the public records that show what surveillance technology is being used, what it costs, and what rules govern it.

Tell us where to look.

You don't need to know whether your community uses Flock cameras, and you don't need to track down the records yourself. Give us the community or agency name and we'll take it from there.

What community should we look into?

City, county, sheriff's department, police department, or another local government agency. If you're not sure exactly who operates the system, that's okay.

We only ask for information needed to research the request. Your email is optional and is used only if you want an update.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

A request starts the research, not an accusation.

We'll determine which agency may hold the relevant records, submit public records requests when appropriate, and review what we receive before publishing information about the community.

Records can take time to obtain. A community appearing in our research queue does not mean we've concluded anything about how its surveillance technology is being used.
